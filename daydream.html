Project Daydream

When does wandering thought become productive exploration?

A Hermean research program with its own question and evidence requirements. Capability definitions and evaluation remain under development.

Working scopeNext questionsResearch records

Working scope

Spontaneous internally generated cognition, including associative drift, mind-wandering, incubation, unexpected cross-domain connections, and potentially useful idea emergence without immediate external task demand.

Unresolved boundaries

These phenomena are not presumed to share one mechanism or to be uniformly productive. Random output, extra search, and deliberate imagination are alternative explanations to distinguish.

What comes next

  1. Define productive off-task cognition and its evidence requirements.
  2. Distinguish spontaneous cognition from adjacent phenomena.
  3. Determine whether a meaningful computational operationalization is possible.

Research direction · Not an approved experiment
