# Project Fiction — Decision Gates

Status: exploratory

Epistemic label: NEW PROPOSAL

Updated: 2026-10-12

These human construct/signal gates operationalize the handover's preference for cheap falsification. No human or empirical capability gate has passed. Offline checker verification is separately scoped. Numeric thresholds, domains, modality, sample sizes, effect sizes, and budgets must be specified before the relevant data are collected or examined; this document does not invent them.

| Gate | Evidence required for continuation | Failure / modification trigger | Next rational decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G0 — Construct and information boundary | A defensible transformation criterion, unit of observation, and documented external information constraint. | Definition admits paraphrase/randomness without meaningful transformation, or "fixed" conditions cannot be stated. | Narrow/split the target or redefine the boundary before detector work. |
| G1 — Label feasibility | Independent rubric, documented evidence access, reliability estimate with uncertainty, and distinct validity checks for process and product criteria. | Labels rely on telemetry/features, condition instructions, Aha alone, or superficial output cues. | Revise the evidence/rubric; consider dimensions/coarser windows. Do not train on invalid labels. |
| G2 — Directional local signal | Participant/session-disjoint evidence that candidate features add useful information beyond agreed nuisance/output baselines and negative controls. | Leakage, nuisance-only equivalence, unstable labels, or no useful incremental signal under adequate measurement. | Revise signal/instrument or stop the detector branch. An inconclusive small test is not proof of no capability. |
| G3 — Same-modality domain transfer | A model and threshold locked using Domain A, tested on untouched Domain B with clear domain difference and participant controls. | Transfer is absent, uncertain, or explained by shared cue/identity/difficulty distributions. | Narrow the transfer claim or redesign; retain a runtime proposal only with an independent rationale. |
| G4 — Confirmatory study | Preregistered construct, analysis, outcome, and meaningful effect; sample justified for power/precision and clustering; independent confirmation data. | Frozen criteria are not met or alternative explanations survive. | Modify/abandon the specific claim; preserve negative and inconclusive evidence. |
| GR — Runtime branch | Independent model-task improvement from detector-guided intervention beyond matched interventions, with false-trigger costs measured. | No incremental outcome benefit or unacceptable tradeoffs. | Revise or abandon runtime use independently of G3. |
| GW — Reward branch | Independent capability improvement, robustness to noise/shift, and explicit tests for feature gaming and proxy optimization. | Higher reward without target improvement, exploitability, or unstable out-of-distribution behavior. | Reject/narrow reward use; accuracy alone never licenses production RL. |

G0-G3 are directional feasibility/continuation gates, not confirmation. Passing a gate changes the next research decision, not the evidential label of untested downstream claims. GR and GW are separate branches; human signal evidence also does not establish human-to-model transfer. Modality translation requires a later separate hypothesis and design.

For each gate, record the hypothesis, operational definition, alternative explanation, control, success criterion, failure criterion, and next decision before execution. Link any actual evidence to the [experiment registry](../../experiments/registry.md) and [hypothesis map](hypotheses.md).
