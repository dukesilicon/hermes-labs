Research to date

What we’ve done so far

  • Built a working evaluator that checks proposed solutions against explicit task rules. Inspect the working evaluator.
  • Ran an offline rehearsal where ordinary search reproduced gains we might otherwise have attributed to reframing. Read the PT-001 rehearsal.
  • Found that the two model-evaluation source worlds allowed 2 and 13 valid plans, respectively, and specified a comparison within one world. Read the matching audit.
  • Developed and checked candidate human-study materials. Internal review found that the latest fictional-world task’s teaching comes close to giving away a solution.

The proposals address separate measurement questions and can be supported independently. The work completed so far consists of instrument development, offline checks and research design.

Researcher

Bright · Researcher, Hermes Labs
Abuja, Nigeria

Bright is an independent researcher with a background in health and a self-taught grounding in machine learning and data science. A longstanding curiosity about machines developed into an interest in how minds construct ideas, reason and act. His engagement with language models and philosophy prompted the questions behind Project Fiction. At Hermes Labs, he investigates human cognitive capabilities and how their functions might be reproduced in artificial systems.
