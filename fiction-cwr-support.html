Project Fiction · Proposed $5,000 phase

Build a fairer model comparison

If two demonstrations show actions in a different order, can we keep the underlying task and its opportunities for success the same?

Research to date

What we’ve done so far

We built a closed-world recombination evaluator that checks plans against explicit rules. Our audit found that the two source worlds in a proposed comparison allowed 2 and 13 valid plans. A difference in model performance could therefore reflect the underlying task instead of what the demonstration showed.

Inspect the working evaluator. Read the matching audit.

Scope

What the phase would deliver

  • A bounded audit of demonstration pairs that share a source world, goal, rules and set of actions, with a record of qualifying and rejected comparisons.
  • An independently implemented validation check, including whether a target can be solved by copying and whether the order actually establishes which inputs were used.
  • Two independent methodological reviews and a report recommending whether to advance to a separately scoped model pilot, narrow the comparison or stop.

This phase tests whether the comparison can be built and interpreted. It covers offline audit and review. Human participation and model runs fall outside its scope.
