Project Affect

How does affect regulate what a cognitive system attends to and does?

A Hermean research program with its own question and evidence requirements. Capability definitions and evaluation remain under development.

Working scopeNext questionsResearch records

Working scope

Emotion and affect as regulatory mechanisms of cognition: shaping attention, salience, valuation, memory, motivation, risk perception, and action selection.

Unresolved boundaries

The target is measurable synthetic regulation. Emotional self-description does not establish regulation, biological equivalence, or subjective feeling. A first regulatory target and execution priority remain unresolved.

What comes next

  1. Select a regulatory function and observable measures.
  2. Specify a manipulable mechanism and controls for wording, reward, and extra computation.
  3. Define failure criteria and the next research decision.

Research direction · Not an approved experiment

Research records

The repository records conceptual and foundational work. No Affect implementation, dataset, executed experiment, or empirical result is established here. There is no paper or runnable artifact listed for this program yet.
