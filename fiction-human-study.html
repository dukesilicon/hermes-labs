Project Fiction · Proposed $1,000 contribution

How do you recognize an invention?

Can a task show how someone develops a useful solution from what they have been shown, while leaving room for what they may already know?

The immediate research decision is whether the candidate task can support an independent assessment of useful change. The proposed contribution supports a revision or rejection, written reviews, and a decision about further preparation.

Research to date

What we’ve done so far

Under EXP-0001, we developed written tasks, scoring materials and calibration examples. The proposed assessment separates whether a solution works from what its recorded development shows. For the latest fictional-world task, offline checking confirmed that multiple designs work. Our internal review found that one teaching example comes close to giving away a solution. That is the first design problem this milestone addresses.

Read the EXP-0001 research update.

Scope

What the contribution would deliver

  • One bounded revision of the existing task, or a documented case for rejecting it, with materials, checks and limitations.
  • Two independent written scientific design reviews covering the task’s validity and what conclusions its evidence could support.
  • A response to those reviews, a decision to proceed with preparation, revise or stop, and a cost outline for any justified next phase.

This is a desk review of the research design. Full protocol preparation, recruitment, participant testing and analysis are separately scoped later work.
