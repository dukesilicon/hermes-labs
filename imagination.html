Project Imagination

How do we build and reason about worlds that are not given?

A Hermean research program with its own question and evidence requirements. Capability definitions and evaluation remain under development.

Working scopeNext questionsResearch records

Working scope

Deliberate possible-world construction: generating, manipulating, exploring, and evaluating hypothetical scenarios, futures, counterfactuals, objects, systems, environments, or states.

Unresolved boundaries

The boundary between imagination, prediction, simulation, planning, creativity, and Fiction remains unresolved. Hypothetical prose alone does not establish the capability.

What comes next

  1. Sharpen the capability definition and its boundaries.
  2. Identify tasks that test deliberate construction and manipulation.
  3. Develop hypotheses and independent evaluation criteria.

Research direction · Not an approved experiment
