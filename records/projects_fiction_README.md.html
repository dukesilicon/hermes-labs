Scientific record

Role: Active first project and primary research program of Hermes Labs.

Working definition

Innovation under fixed informational conditions: the ability to recombine, restructure, or transform already-available concepts, relations, memories, or representations in a novel way to produce a new actionable concept, strategy, interpretation, or solution, without materially new external information entering the system.

Shorthand: same information state -> novel recombination/restructuring -> new actionable representation. What remains fixed must be operationalized; internal representations can change. Retrieval is part of the substrate. The contrast is mere reuse or reproduction versus innovative transformation, not a binary opposition between construction and retrieval.

Project Fiction is unfinished and under active stress-testing. Develop, attack, narrow, modify, test, and falsify hypotheses; preserve what changes and what survives. Do not defend a claim because it belongs to the current project.

Current research direction

Observe and operationalize the human capability; test whether innovation events or dimensions can be identified independently; investigate behavioral, linguistic, acoustic, semantic, or other accompanying signals while controlling difficulty, uncertainty, hesitation, retrieval effort, wording novelty, and cognitive load.

The proposed Invention Classifier is a research instrument, not invention itself. Phase One prioritizes domain generalization with modality held constant. Modality translation comes later. A directional pre-test asks whether further resources are warranted; a small success does not confirm the thesis.

Runtime assistance and reward-signal use are separate hypotheses. Detector performance establishes neither intervention benefit nor reward suitability. Ultimately, investigate reproduction or encouragement of the capability in AI models, first assessing relevant behavior in existing systems.

Fiction-CWR v0.1 operationalizes the narrow branch FH-08 with P primitive probes, R supplied-handbook retrieval, E execution and primary F familiar versus C withheld synthesis.
