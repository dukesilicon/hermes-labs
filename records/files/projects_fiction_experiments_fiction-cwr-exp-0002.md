## Seven required statements
1. **Hypothesis:** Pinned frontier models may show a substantive familiar-to-withheld composition exact-validity loss despite independently demonstrated primitive competence (FH-08; complementary to FH-01/FH-07).
2. **Operational definition:** Executable solutions in finite W=(S,A,T,s0,G,C,B), scored by conjunction of strict schema, supplied vocabulary/effects, legal transitions, invariants, complete goal and budget. Novelty relative to supplied demonstrations, with necessary structural withholding certified or claim narrowed.
3. **Alternative explanation:** Primitive/spec misunderstanding, generic search difficulty, memory/load, output burden, familiar surfaces, evidence imbalance, prompt/budget, mastery selection or defective graders.
4. **Control:** Independent P, finite-handbook R, E execution, primary paired F/C, independent checker/oracle, copy/random/greedy baselines, representation and higher-budget diagnostics; all task/model outcomes retained.
5. **Success criterion:** Directional pilot may justify confirmation only after measurement/matching gates; independent confirmation supports the scoped substantive loss if its prespecified lower bound reaches the frozen deficit in a qualified model and confounds are addressed.
6. **Failure criterion:** Upper bound below the chosen loss rejects that scoped size; crossing is inconclusive. Failed P prevents the primitive-understood attribution; defective matching/grading prevents intended interpretation. Strong C and null/reversed effects are preserved.
7. **Next decision:** Complete stimulus/checker/model/budget freeze, execute only authorized pilot, then narrow/reject or design independently powered confirmation. No detector/RL or runtime/reward adoption follows.
