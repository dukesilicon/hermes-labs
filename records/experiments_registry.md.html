Where records belong

Project-specific designs and runs belong in projects/<project>/experiments/. Cross-program experiments belong in experiments/shared/ and must state their relevance to each participating project. Link evaluations, model configurations, datasets, and results from the complete experiment record rather than maintaining conflicting copies.

Future registry entries

Give each experiment a stable identifier and register its project or shared scope, question or linked hypothesis, actual status, design-record path, and any run/result paths. Record relevant dates and changes in status. Register actual documented proposals, clearly marking incomplete designs; do not turn placeholder identifiers or speculative mentions into experiments.

Use proposed, underway, completed, blocked, or cancelled according to the actual state. A proposed design is not an executed run. Completion does not imply success; preserve negative and inconclusive results. If a design changes across runs, identify the version used for each run.

Complete design and run records

Record the applicable details needed to interpret and repeat the work:

  • Research question or hypothesis, design rationale, conditions, controls, and intended evaluation criteria.
  • Inputs, dataset identity and version, selection and preparation steps, and relevant information constraints.
  • Model identity and version, configuration, environment or dependencies, prompts, and random seeds where relevant.
  • Procedure or linked scripts, actual run conditions, and deviations from the design.
  • Metrics and how they were computed, outputs and artifact locations, and the run's actual state.
  • Observations, Hermes' interpretation, limitations, uncertainty, and unresolved questions.

Separate proposed methods from executed conditions and observed results. Mark unavailable or inapplicable details explicitly. Prefer scripts and configuration over undocumented manual steps; explain reproduction limits when they remain. Substantive design or evaluation changes must update canonical project documents and the research log (not included in this public selection: ../logs/research-log.md).
