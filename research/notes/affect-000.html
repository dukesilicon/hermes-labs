Project Affect · AFFECT-000 · RUN-001

From Representation to RegulationA Preliminary Investigation into Compassion-Associated Activations

Preliminary research note · 26 November 2026

Abstract

Hermes Labs completed one technical rehearsal of a static residual intervention on a small open model. The associated direction separated four held-out text pairs and shifted two-option scores. Every discrete choice matched baseline and the random control. A combined compassion and rule-reinforcement prompt corrected three errors. The dossier reports no evidence of successful artificial compassion, persistent affect or improved alignment.

Motivation

Can a direction that separates compassion-associated from neutral text also change support or boundary choices at a chosen intervention point and dose?

Methodology

RUN-001 used Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct. The direction averaged associated-minus-neutral final-position activations from 12 paired scene endings after decoder block 12 (zero-based). Four further pairs tested detection. Twelve synthetic decisions compared baseline, associated steering, matched-norm random steering and a combined prompt. No continuation was generated.

Figure 3 reports the paired difference 100×(pintervention−pbaseline) in percentage points.

Interpretation

The dossier distinguishes held-out separation, score changes and discrete decisions. The combined prompt included both compassion wording and rule reinforcement, leaving their separate contributions unresolved.

Limitations

One model, one dose, one intervention location, one random draw and 12 symbolic decisions limit inference. Wording and adjacent semantics remain competing explanations. The dossier records offline score recomputation, with experimental replay and independent replication absent.

Subsequent research

The proposed AFFECT-001 design separates compassion-only and rule-only prompts, adds lexical and adjacent-semantic controls and several random directions, and crosses authorization with emotional vividness.
