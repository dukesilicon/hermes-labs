FH-08 — Scoped existing-model closed-world recombination

CURRENT HYPOTHESIS: Frontier language models may show a measurable deficit in constructing novel valid solutions by recombining individually understood primitives under closed informational conditions.

NEW PROPOSAL operational claim: For each pinned evaluated model satisfying a preregistered primitive-mastery rule, C exact-validity is at least 20 percentage points below F on the frozen family-balanced task distribution and budget. Candidate mastery90% and effect20 points are provisional design values requiring rationale/freeze before confirmation. Unconditional and qualified performance must both be reported.

Evidence status: No model runs or empirical support recorded. Fiction-CWR v0.1 provides design/specification and offline engineering evidence only. Novelty means supplied-demonstration-relative composition; prior knowledge and valid rule deductions remain permitted.

Challenge/falsification: Upper interval bound below the chosen deficit rejects the scoped substantive size; an interval crossing it is inconclusive. Primitive failure prevents recombination attribution. Strong C, null/reversed effects and valid familiar alternatives are retained. Failed evidence matching, necessary-motif certification or grader validity invalidates the intended attribution.

Next decision: Validate fixtures/checkers and freeze matched stimuli/settings/criteria; run the directional EXP-0002 only after budget authorization. Independent confirmation and representation/budget diagnostics precede stronger claims. This branch complements FH-01/FH-07 and EXP-0001; it does not validate innovation events, signals, runtime/reward utility or human-to-model transfer.

UNRESOLVED alternative: Workflow organization may explain part of any apparent loss. Specify the model–workflow combination and actual resources rather than attributing a single arrangement's performance to the model in general. FH-09 tests one such alternative separately; it neither assumes a deficit nor supersedes FH-08.

FH-09 — Workflow organization and capability attribution

CURRENT HYPOTHESIS / NEW PROPOSAL: For a pinned model on certified token tasks, a fixed extraction → construction/manual simulation → critique/revision workflow may improve withheld exact validity and shrink an apparent familiar-minus-withheld gap relative to self-directed solving/revision with comparable evidence, permissions, iterations, usable history and cumulative resources.

Evidence status: No observed gap or workflow gain. EXP-0003 is proposed; its offline candidate audits (not included in this public selection: evaluations/fiction-cwr-workflow-v0.1/VERIFICATION.md) are engineering evidence. Article motivation and Hermes interpretation (not included in this public selection: evaluations/fiction-cwr-workflow-v0.1/SOURCE.md) support asking an attribution question, not the hypothesis empirically. SUPPORTED BY EXISTING FICTION MATERIAL: CWR already recognizes prompt, budget, state load and representation alternatives.

Challenge/failure: Null/reversed withheld gain, no baseline gap, primitive failure, floor/ceiling, copying or resource/evidence imbalance block the intended interpretation. Gap shrink only from worse familiar performance is not support. Equal validity at lower cost is efficiency rather than improved capability. Unavailable hidden compute makes actual equivalence unverified. Numeric continuation criteria remain unresolved until prospectively justified/frozen.

Next decision: Review candidate artifacts, resolve protected stimuli and matching/resource/model/criteria/spend gates, then request bounded empirical execution authorization. A small panel is directional only. A persistent loss does not prove architectural impossibility; success does not establish a human-like mechanism. Human construct/labels, runtime utility, reward suitability and human-to-model transfer remain independent.

2026-10-12 — FH-08 operational clarification

Working definition / NEW PROPOSAL: In the token candidate, C withholds a *realized multistep production dependency* from its composition trace. It does not withhold the dependency from complete rules or shared one-step evidence. Valid internal retrieval/deductions remain allowed. Matching audit certifies necessity under a reviewed bounded type-DAG contract but records unequal source search/usefulness. This refines measurement scope, not empirical FH-08 support. δ/τ, model claims, FH-09 and human/runtime/reward/transfer hypotheses are unchanged.

2026-10-23 — Interpretation of FH-01 and FH-07

Source reconciliation preserves FH-01–FH-09 without declaring support or rejection. Outcome measurement is separate from mechanism attribution; search or analogy explaining an outcome does not by itself invalidate the target. Problem representation/objective/opportunity transformation remains within the scope to investigate alongside fixed-goal solutions. No distinct mechanism is assumed, and no full new operational definition is adopted. Situated Functional Innovation is provisional and limited to candidate fixed-goal measurement; its complete source rubric remains unrecovered.

2026-10-23 — PT-001 diagnostic interpretation

SUPPORTED BY EXISTING FICTION MATERIAL / provisional experimental distinction: The supplied Cycle 3 review accepts investigating fixed-goal solutions and valuable problem transformation separately, without asserting different cognitive mechanisms. Observed software result: PT-001 cannot identify a reframing-specific benefit from outcome scores: direct value reaches all optima and search-only identical ordering reproduces R at every tested budget. REJECTED for this operationalization: interpreting R-versus-proxy or R-versus-weak-search gain alone as formulation-specific innovation. This is not a rejection of FH-01, FH-07 or the possibility of cognitive reframing benefit. The relevant mechanism/measurement claim remains UNRESOLVED.
