## Accepted methodological direction

**SUPPORTED BY EXISTING FICTION MATERIAL:** Outcome-based innovation measurement and the mechanism producing an outcome are separate questions. The source user explicitly accepts this distinction. Define the phenomenon independently before interpreting search, analogy, recombination, restructuring, or their coordination as its explanation. These processes may constitute the capability; Fiction does not require a unique cognitive primitive.

The canonical [research question](research-question.md) and working definition remain unchanged. CWR remains one experimental branch, not Fiction's definition. Human construct/label/signal, model capability, runtime usefulness, reward suitability, and transfer retain separate evidence requirements.

**Working direction:** Investigate both (A) novel functional solutions to an externally supplied goal and (B) valuable transformation of the problem representation, objective, or actionable opportunity. These are measurement scopes to investigate, not established faculties or replacements for EXP-0001/2/3. They also do not rename the runtime/reward branches, which earlier conversation text called A/B.

## Measurement obligations

Distinguish four variables: externally supplied information; initial goal; participant/system problem representation; and permitted operations plus actual environmental constraints. Representation change does not itself introduce external facts or authorize rewriting the world's rules. Record what is held fixed, what is manipulated, and residual differences.

Keep novelty relative to demonstrations, individual prior knowledge, model pretraining, and wider knowledge separate. Supplied-evidence novelty does not establish novelty in pretraining or in the world. In finite worlds, rule-valid feasibility alone does not establish broader usefulness. Functional reproduction in AI and human-like mechanism require different evidence.

For problem transformation, justify value independently of the actor's chosen goal. Avoid rewarding easier self-selected goals, arbitrary substitution, or constraint violations. Control unequal search freedom and difficulty. A representation change with no observable behavioral consequence cannot establish benefit merely from its description. Whether delayed or currently unmeasurable benefits can be studied defensibly remains open.

## Reconciliation and next decision

The early conversation centered the classifier and a construction-versus-retrieval distinction. Its later handover and the current canon supersede that ontology with retrieval as substrate and reuse versus transformation. Preserve the early formulation as history, not a revived label scheme.

The first-cycle report's phrasing about distinguishing innovation from ordinary search can suggest that a different mechanism is required. The subsequent user clarification explicitly removes that requirement. Outcome validity and mechanistic explanation must be tested separately.

The pilot's existing operational retrieval/deduction/recombination comparisons remain useful controls. They do not license excluding an otherwise qualifying outcome merely because search, deduction, or analogy produced it. Whether the pilot's measures capture more than constrained problem solving remains an unresolved construct-validity question.
